What do the columns and setups mean?
  • Score: weight of the setups matched, plus how close the stock is to its breakout level. Out of 100.
  • Level: the 20-day high. A close above it is the breakout trigger.
  • Stop: the 20-day low (bottom of the base). A reference only, not advice.
  • Vol ratio: 5-day average volume / 50-day average. Below 0.85 means volume is drying up.
  • BB %ile: where the Bollinger Band width sits in the last 120 days. Under 10 means a squeeze.
  • Consolidation 20-day range under 12% with volume drying up.
  • 52W High within 5% of the 52-week high, above the 50-day average (uptrend).
  • Squeeze Bollinger volatility in the lowest 10% of the last 120 days.
  • Base 25%+ below the 52-week high, within 20% of the 52-week low, now forming a tight base.
  • On smaller screens less important columns hide automatically. On mobile every stock is shown as a card.

For educational screening only, not trading advice. "Pre-breakout" does not mean a breakout will happen. Decide your own stop-loss and risk.
