Columns ka matlab aur setups
  • Score: setups ke weight + breakout level ke kitna paas hai. 100 mein se.
  • Level: pichhle 20 din ka high. Isse upar close = breakout trigger.
  • Stop: pichhle 20 din ka low (base ka neeche). Sirf reference hai.
  • Vol ratio: 5-din avg volume / 50-din avg. 0.85 se kam = volume sookh raha hai (dry-up).
  • BB %ile: Bollinger width pichhle 120 din mein kitne percentile par. 10 se kam = squeeze.
  • Consolidation: 20 din ki range 12% se kam aur volume dry-up.
  • 52W High: high se 5% ke andar aur 50-DMA ke upar (uptrend).
  • Squeeze: Bollinger volatility sabse kam 10% mein.
  • Base: 52W high se 25%+ gira, low se 20% ke andar, ab tight base (Dhanuka jaisa).
  • Chhote screen par kam zaroori columns apne aap chhup jate hain. Mobile par har stock card mein dikhta hai.

Sirf educational screening hai, trade ki salah nahi. Pre-breakout ka matlab ye nahi ki breakout aayega. Stop-loss aur risk apna khud tay karo.
