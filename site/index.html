Columns ka matlab aur setups
  • Score: setups ke weight + breakout level ke kitna paas hai. 100 mein se.
  • Level: pichhle 20 din ka high. Isse upar close = breakout trigger.
  • Stop: pichhle 20 din ka low (base ka neeche). Sirf reference hai.
  • Vol ratio: 5-din avg volume / 50-din avg. 0.85 se kam = volume sookh raha hai (dry-up).
  • BB pctile: Bollinger width pichhle 120 din mein kitne percentile par. 10 se kam = squeeze.
  • Consolidation + Volume dry-up: 20 din ki range 12% se kam aur volume dry-up.
  • 52W High ke paas: high se 5% ke andar aur 50-DMA ke upar (uptrend).
  • Bollinger squeeze: volatility sabse kam 10% mein.
  • Beaten-down base: 52W high se 25%+ gira, low se 20% ke andar, ab tight base (Dhanuka jaisa).

Sirf educational screening hai, trade ki salah nahi. Pre-breakout ka matlab ye nahi ki breakout aayega. Stop-loss aur risk apna khud tay karo. Data: Yahoo Finance (daily, end-of-day).
